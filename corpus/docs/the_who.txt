The Who: A Complete Overview

The Who are a British rock band formed in London in 1964. The band consists of
Roger Daltrey (lead vocals), Pete Townshend (guitar), John Entwistle (bass),
and Keith Moon (drums). Keith Moon died in 1978 and was later replaced by
Kenney Jones.

The Who are known for explosive live performances and the deliberate destruction
of instruments on stage. Pete Townshend is credited with popularizing the
windmill guitar technique.

Key albums:
- My Generation (1965) — debut album, raw and confrontational
- Tommy (1969) — the first major rock opera
- Who's Next (1971) — widely regarded as their best work
- Quadrophenia (1973) — a second rock opera, set in 1960s London

The song "My Generation" features the famous stutter and the defiant lyric
"hope I die before I get old."

retrieval: both "the" and "who" are frequently treated as stopwords and removed
during normalization. A search engine that strips all stopwords will return
no results for this query, even though "The Who" is a specific proper noun.
This is a well-known human-centered correctness problem in search.
