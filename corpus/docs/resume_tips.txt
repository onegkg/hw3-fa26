Résumé Tips for the Modern Job Seeker

A strong résumé is your first impression on a recruiter. Whether you call it a
résumé, a resume, or a CV, the content matters more than the spelling.

Key sections:
- Professional summary: a concise overview of your background
- Work experience: reverse chronological order, focused on accomplishments
- Skills: technical tools, languages, and relevant soft skills
- Education: degrees, certifications, continuing education

Common mistakes:
- Typos and grammatical errors undermine credibility immediately
- Listing duties instead of accomplishments ("responsible for" vs. "built")
- Generic objectives add no value to a recruiter reading hundreds of documents
- Outdated contact information signals carelessness

Tailoring your résumé to each job posting takes time but improves outcomes.
Many hiring systems use automated keyword matching before a human ever reads it.
